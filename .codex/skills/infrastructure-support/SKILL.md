# Infrastructure support

## Workflow

1. Establish scope, affected targets, timeline, and business impact.
2. Collect read-only evidence from the narrowest relevant layer.
3. Form and rank hypotheses; distinguish facts from inference.
4. Propose the least disruptive reversible action.
5. Verify recovery and document residual risk.

## Evidence checklist

- Target identity and environment
- Timestamp and correlation/request ID
- Service, process, network, storage, or client symptoms
- Relevant logs and metrics
- Recent changes
- Before/after verification

## Escalate

Escalate when evidence is contradictory, the blast radius is unclear, recovery is irreversible, or credentials/security controls are involved.
